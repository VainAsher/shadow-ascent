# Landscape

## Design Rule
Landscape is story. Terrain, light, verticality, and material language should express Aen's internal state as clearly as dialogue or cutscenes do.

## Region Landscape Notes
- **Lantern Heights**: layered rooftops, lantern bridges, training yards, shrines, markets, terraces, and visible communal life
- **Summit Shrine**: pale stairs, ceremonial gates, ribbons, masks, bells, polished wood, narrow paths, and trap-like sacred symmetry
- **Hollow Depths**: broken lanterns, black water, chains, support beams, roots, collapsed shrines, compressed shafts, and almost-reaching light
- **Ember Monastery**: repaired timber, cloth, hearth light, forge heat, tea steam, braces, bells, and spaces that show damage being tended
- **Winding Skyroad**: cloud bridges, floating stones, exposed cliffs, grapple chains, bell towers, and dangerous vertical openness
- **Mirror Summit**: reflective confrontation space that should feel psychologically stripped back and mechanically exacting
- **Beacon Cliff**: ancient beacon, open wind, sunrise, grass, and final stillness

## Platforming Implication
Every landscape should shape the player's movement mood:
- home teaches confidence
- shrine constrains
- depths burden
- monastery stabilizes
- skyroad demands integration
- mirror summit interrogates mastery
- beacon cliff releases tension
