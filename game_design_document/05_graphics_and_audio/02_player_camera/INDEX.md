# Player Camera Index

## Checklist
- [ ] Define the default camera framing, follow behavior, and look-ahead expectations
- [ ] Note any camera changes during boss fights, set pieces, or vertical traversal sequences
- [ ] Keep camera behavior tied to readability, not just cinematic ambition
- [ ] Record constraints created by minimap, UI, and platforming precision

## Drift Risks
- Overly dramatic camera behavior will damage platforming trust
- Underdefined camera rules will produce inconsistent feel across regions and encounters
