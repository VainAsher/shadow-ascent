# Game Music

## Musical Direction
The music should follow the emotional campaign as clearly as the world and story do. It should move from warmth and belonging into hollow ritual, grief, support, ascent, confrontation, and final stillness.

## Region Music Reading
- **Lantern Heights**: warm, communal, lantern-lit, alive, but capable of carrying early unease
- **Summit Shrine**: sacred, beautiful, sparse, controlled, and increasingly wrong
- **Hollow Depths**: heavy, spacious, cold, restrained, and grief-soaked
- **Ember Monastery**: warm but not naive; restorative, structured, and human
- **Winding Skyroad**: high, exposed, wind-carried, and earned
- **Mirror Summit**: intimate, tense, reflective, and spiritually unstable
- **Beacon Cliff**: simple, open, sacred, and releasing

## Usage Philosophy
Music should not wallpaper every moment. Silence, reduced scoring, or sparse motifs should be used intentionally, especially in grief or pressure spaces. The score should feel like emotional witness, not constant emotional instruction.

## Leitmotif Potential
The project is well suited to recurring motif use:
- Aen motif
- Yin motif
- Yang motif
- false-praise / Siren motif
- support / return motif
- Beacon motif

These should evolve across the campaign rather than appear as fixed identity tags.

## Current Limitation
The repo does not yet implement the final music system. This section defines the desired music language, not proven runtime behavior.
