# Levels

## Current Structure
The project has two level scales at once:

1. a currently playable bounded route used for QA and feel validation
2. a larger campaign structure defined through plateaus, beats, areas, region fragments, and section templates

## Campaign Level Reading
Levels should be authored as emotional architecture:
- Lantern Heights teaches belonging, movement, and social navigation while quietly introducing isolation.
- Summit Shrine narrows, whitens, symmetrizes, and traps.
- Hollow Depths compresses space and weight to slow recovery into survivable pieces.
- Ember Monastery opens into practice, support, and disciplined rebuilding.
- Winding Skyroad turns the full moveset into exposed ascent.
- Mirror Summit transforms the player's recovered kit into confrontation with the self.
- Beacon Cliff strips complexity back for a quiet, sacred ending space.

## Level Taxonomy
- critical route spaces
- optional and elastic spaces
- social and training spaces
- grief and survival spaces
- recovery and mentorship spaces
- ascent and confrontation spaces
- witness and ending spaces

## GDD Rule
Do not document levels as only obstacle courses. Each level family should answer:
- what Aen feels here
- what the player learns here
- what part of the story changes here
- what ability or relationship becomes newly meaningful here
