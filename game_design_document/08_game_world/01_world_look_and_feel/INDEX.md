# World Look And Feel Index

## Checklist
- [ ] Define the overall world identity beyond individual regions
- [ ] Keep the world framed as Aen's inner life turned into explorable space
- [ ] Clarify how sacred, domestic, ruined, and skybound spaces differ visually and emotionally
- [ ] Record any shift that would change the world from symbolic journey to generic fantasy backdrop

## Drift Risks
- If the world loses its emotional metaphor, the level plan becomes much less distinctive
- If every space is equally stylized, the contrast between home, hollowing, and ascent will blur
