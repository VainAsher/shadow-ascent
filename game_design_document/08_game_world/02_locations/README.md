# Locations

## Design Rule
The world map is not just a collection of biomes. It is Aen's inner life made architectural. Each major location should feel emotionally distinct, mechanically purposeful, and narratively necessary.

## Primary Locations
- **Lantern Heights**: warm mountain settlement of layered rooftops, lantern bridges, training yards, shrines, markets, and social terraces
- **Summit Shrine**: red gates, pale stone stairs, prayer ribbons, ceremonial lanterns, masks, bells, and polished shrine wood that gradually reveal trap-like symmetry and cold beauty
- **The Hollow Depths**: compressed, cold, damp, heavy underworld of broken lanterns, black water, cracked statues, abandoned beams, chains, roots, and almost-light
- **Ember Monastery**: repaired wood, ember orange cloth, tea steam, training bells, braces, forge heat, and chosen warmth
- **The Winding Skyroad**: cloud bridges, suspended ruins, floating stones, bell towers, grapple chains, wall-cling cliffs, and star-wind fields
- **Mirror Summit**: confrontation space where selfhood, release, and reflection collapse into boss architecture
- **Beacon Cliff**: ancient beacon, open wind, simple sacred stone, sunrise, and final witness

## Location Philosophy
Each region should carry:
- an emotional phase
- a platforming and traversal identity
- a distinct light state
- a narrative function in Aen's recovery arc
