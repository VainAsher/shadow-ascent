# Backstory

## Foundational Premise
Aen is a young ninja born with two living spirit-orbs, Yin and Yang. Where most people carry one spiritual polarity, Aen carries both. He is not half-finished and he is not waiting to be completed by someone else. The hidden truth of the story is that he was more than whole from the beginning.

## Emotional Blind Spot
The tragedy is not that Aen lacks power. The tragedy is that he misunderstands himself. He mistakes praise for love, being chosen for being safe, and isolation for destiny. That flaw gives the Veil Maiden a way into his life long before she openly becomes his enemy.

## Pre-Wound World
The story begins in Lantern Heights, where Aen still has:
- Yin, the silver spirit of stillness, intuition, softness, and restraint
- Yang, the golden spirit of courage, action, boldness, and fire
- a real community of friends, mentors, merchants, smiths, elders, and villagers

This matters because the campaign is not about acquiring meaning from nothing. It is about losing touch with what was already true, then learning to live from that truth again without undoing the wound.

## Structural Constraint
After the scripted loss at Summit Shrine, Yin and Yang must not return as ordinary companions. Their later presence is symbolic, distant, remembered, or star-like. The story becomes stronger when it refuses easy reversal.
