# Main Plot

## Canonical Story Sentence
Aen begins surrounded by warmth, is seduced by praise, isolated from community, stripped of Yin and Yang, cast into grief, rebuilt through practical support and brotherhood, climbs with every recovered part of himself, faces the hollow version of his own pain, releases it, and lights a beacon toward a future that can hold loss without being ruled by it.

## Emotional Spine
The full campaign should be read as:

`home -> praise -> isolation -> collapse -> numb survival -> grief -> support -> rebuilding -> ascent -> release -> future`

That is the real plot structure. The world map is not just a route through biomes. It is Aen's inner life translated into level design and progression.

## Campaign Arc
Aen starts in Lantern Heights, where belonging, admiration, and community still feel intact. The Veil Maiden flatters him into believing that separation is destiny, drawing him toward Summit Shrine. There, praise curdles into possession, the Siren of Masks reveals herself, and Yin and Yang are taken from him in a scripted emotional defeat.

He falls into the Hollow Depths, where survival comes before healing and each regained movement tool reflects a small return of agency rather than triumphant restoration. Ember Monastery then reframes recovery as structure, discipline, support, and chosen care. From there, the Winding Skyroad tests whether everything Aen has relearned can be integrated into ascent rather than denial.

At Mirror Summit, the true final enemy is not the Siren but the Hollow Reflection: Aen's own wound wearing his shape. The canonical resolution is release, not domination. At Beacon Cliff, the ending refuses cheap reversal. Yin and Yang remain stars. Aen becomes whole not because everything is restored, but because he can live, love, wait, build, and shine anyway.

## Core Theme
This is not a story about "saving the spirits" or returning life to a perfect earlier state. It is about becoming whole without possessing what was lost and surviving what the wound tried to make permanent.
