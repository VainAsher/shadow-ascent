# Cutscenes Index

## Checklist
- [ ] Define when non-interactive sequences are necessary and when gameplay should carry the story instead
- [ ] Reserve cutscene weight for the Siren reveal, Hollowing, major returns, release, and Beacon beats
- [ ] Clarify how cutscenes should transition in and out of player control
- [ ] Keep emotional clarity ahead of exposition density

## Drift Risks
- Too many cutscenes will work against the embodied nature of the story
- Too little authored staging can leave major beats under-expressed
