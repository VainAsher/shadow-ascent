# Plot Progression

## Act Structure
- Lantern Heights: home before the wound
- Summit Shrine: praise becomes possession
- Hollow Depths: grief given architecture
- Ember Monastery: support becomes structure
- Winding Skyroad: ascent with everything earned
- Mirror Summit: confrontation with the Hollow Reflection
- Beacon Cliff: wholeness without reversal

## Progression Rule
Each major region should do three jobs at once:
- advance the story
- deepen Aen's emotional state
- justify a specific layer of mechanical recovery or mastery

## Region-to-Emotion Flow
- Lantern Heights establishes warmth, belonging, admiration, and early vulnerability to flattery.
- Summit Shrine converts sacred promise into isolation, illusion, and the central wound.
- Hollow Depths slows the pace and forces survival before healing.
- Ember Monastery proves that recovery comes through structure, mentorship, and community.
- Winding Skyroad turns restored abilities into integrated ascent.
- Mirror Summit forces Aen to face the version of himself built out of pain.
- Beacon Cliff resolves the story through release, witness, and future-facing light rather than reunion fantasy.

## Narrative Guardrails
- The Siren of Masks causes the wound but is not the deepest enemy.
- The Hollow Reflection is the true final boss because the deepest enemy is what the wound becomes inside Aen.
- Yin and Yang must not be returned as a cheap reward state at the end.
- The best ending should align acceptance, restraint, and release with the strongest thematic resolution.
