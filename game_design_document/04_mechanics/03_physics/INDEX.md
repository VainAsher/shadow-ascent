# Physics Index

## Checklist
- [ ] State the intended feel of gravity, inertia, fall control, collision, knockback, and traversal forgiveness
- [ ] Clarify where realism matters and where responsiveness should override it
- [ ] Tie physics expectations to platforming readability, combat pacing, and emotional tone
- [ ] Note any special-case regional physics or boss arena exceptions that need deliberate justification

## Drift Risks
- Inconsistent movement physics will damage trust in traversal-heavy regions
- Overly simulation-heavy rules can undermine the readable, authored Metroidvania feel
