# Game Universe

## Core Rule
The universe of Shadow Ascent is not only what the player sees on screen. It is also a contract-driven system of story flags, beat progression, NPC scheduling, world-state pressure, and symbolic restrictions that continue to exist beyond the player's immediate view.

## Systemic Truth
The current project already assumes that the world keeps meaningfully changing outside direct player perception through:
- story flags and beat state
- plateau eligibility and progression rules
- NPC availability and dialogue routing
- mission and side-quest gating
- world, faction, and settlement state contracts
- regional overlays and mutation signals

## Plateau Grammar Layer
The studio pack clarifies the strongest usable model for the wider universe: each major region acts as a narrative plateau with its own grammar. A plateau owns:
- allowed room intents
- allowed enemy families
- allowed motifs and light state
- traversal assumptions
- NPC presence rules
- optional generation constraints
- story truths that elastic content cannot violate

That framing helps the universe read as authored and systemic at the same time.

## Narrative Constraint Layer
The universe is governed by narrative truths that are stronger than momentary player desire. Examples:
- Yin and Yang may be present before the scripted loss, but not restored as ordinary companions afterward.
- The Siren causes the wound, but she is not the final release boss.
- Elastic content may deepen the world, but it must not violate campaign truth.

## Validation Implication
The pack also clarifies that this universe should be treated as rule-checked rather than purely interpretive. Generation or content validation should fail when:
- required abilities are missing for a generated route
- bosses appear in the wrong plateau
- light state contradicts act state
- NPC role state contradicts story flags
- optional content attempts to advance core story truth out of order
- Beacon resolution appears before Hollow Reflection release

## Design Reading
This makes the game universe feel authored and morally structured rather than simulation-noisy. The player can explore, loop, and deepen their experience, but the world is not neutral. It is carrying thematic law.

## Long-Term Direction
The repo's systemic-world ambition means this layer can grow, but the current GDD should treat the universe first as:
- spiritually symbolic
- narratively constrained
- contract-backed
- reactive at the level of social and world-state signals
