# Play Flow

## Core Player Flow
The expected play flow is:

1. enter or return to a socially or emotionally readable space
2. receive direction through NPCs, missions, memory, or environment
3. move into a pressure route or traversal problem
4. use current abilities to explore, fight, and survive
5. hit a challenge, wound, boss, or emotional threshold
6. gain clarity, support, or a restored capability
7. revisit the world with changed movement, changed meaning, or changed relationships

## Moment-to-Moment Flow
On a smaller scale, the player should regularly alternate between:
- traversal
- encounter
- interaction
- reflection
- progression

This keeps the game from collapsing into either pure combat gauntlets or pure dialogue drift.

## Encounter Flow
The desired encounter rhythm is:
1. read terrain and pressure shape
2. identify which movement answers are safe
3. choose restraint, stealth, direct pressure, or escape
4. preserve flow rather than getting pinned into flat-footed trading
5. exit with either control or visible emotional cost

This means combat should usually feel like traversal under threat rather than detached arena DPS rotation.

## Campaign Rhythm
The campaign should repeatedly move through:
- home or support
- pressure or distortion
- collapse or confrontation
- rebuilding or return

That rhythm is one of the game's strongest structural advantages because it ties emotional pacing to movement and mission pacing.

## Expression Flow
The player should also feel a repeating choice between:
- quiet control and non-lethal resolution
- loud commitment and lethal pressure

That choice should matter in stealth viability, noise generation, counter windows, and the emotional reading of late-game resolution.

## Optional Content Flow
Optional quests and elastic spaces should not feel like random filler. They should fit one of a few clear purposes:
- deepen relationships
- expand understanding
- reinforce mastery
- provide recovery space
- add symbolic echoes to the main route

## Endgame Flow Rule
The late-game flow should not become a flat power fantasy. Even when the player has the full kit, the rhythm should still point toward release, witness, and meaning rather than endless domination.

The final confrontation should therefore function as an exam of integrated movement, spatial reading, and emotional discipline, not just the hardest damage race in the game.
