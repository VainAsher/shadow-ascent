# Setting

## Core Setting Statement
The game takes place across a symbolic fantasy mountain world where geography mirrors Aen's inner life. The setting is not just a backdrop for progression. It is the emotional campaign turned into place, light, architecture, and traversal.

## Named Plateau Sequence
- Lantern Heights
- Summit Shrine
- The Hollow Depths
- Ember Monastery
- The Winding Skyroad
- Mirror Summit
- Beacon Cliff

## Setting Tone
The world should move through explicit emotional and light states:
- hope and belonging
- praise and chosen-ness
- isolation and collapse
- numb survival and grief
- support and rebuilding
- ascent and release
- wholeness and future

The current campaign model already tracks these shifts through plateau phases and light states such as warm, hollow, ember, ascendant, and beacon.
