# Audience

## Core Audience
The clearest audience for Shadow Ascent is players who want a narrative-driven 2D action-exploration game where traversal, combat, and story all reinforce each other. These players are likely to respond to:
- strong emotional worldbuilding
- ability-gated movement progression
- symbolic boss design
- a campaign that feels authored rather than infinitely procedural

## Likely Player Tastes
This project is best positioned for players who already like:
- Metroidvania progression and return-loop structure
- side-scrolling action-platformers with precise movement
- story-rich games where place, mechanics, and theme are tightly linked
- games willing to be sad, reflective, and restrained instead of purely power-fantasy driven

## What They Are Buying Into
Players should come to Shadow Ascent for:
- a spiritual mountain-world journey with a strong emotional spine
- movement and recovery systems that feel meaningfully tied to the story
- bosses and regions that represent emotional states instead of generic escalation
- a campaign about wholeness, loss, support, and release rather than simple revenge or restoration

## Comparison Frame
The safest comparison frame is not "this is for every Metroidvania player." It is closer to:
- players who want exploration and movement depth
- players who value authored narrative tone
- players who are open to symbolic and emotionally explicit storytelling

## Current Limitation
The project has strong thematic identity, but the repo does not yet prove market-fit claims beyond design intent. This section should avoid overconfident demographic or commercial language until there is player testing, wishlist data, or external response evidence.

## Research Needed
This section needs external validation work more than internal prose:
- audience comparison research against likely genre peers
- pitch testing on different hook formulations
- wishlist or community response evidence
- playtest evidence about tolerance for emotionally explicit storytelling

## Designer Questions
- What is the primary commercial hook: ninja traversal, emotional healing arc, or both together?
- Which comparison games are closest in actual player expectation, not just designer taste?
- How much symbolic explicitness will your target audience accept before it becomes a sales drag?
