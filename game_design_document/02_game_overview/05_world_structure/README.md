# World Structure

## Core Structure
Shadow Ascent should use a campaign-first world structure built from emotional plateaus rather than a single uninterrupted open world. The player advances through major regions that function as acts in Aen's inner and outer journey.

## Structural Model
- a critical route moves the player through the plateau sequence
- optional and elastic content branches off that route without invalidating story truth
- hubs and support spaces reset emotional tone, social context, and mission clarity
- restored abilities reopen older paths and make later ascent possible

## Plateau Sequence
- Lantern Heights
- Summit Shrine
- Hollow Depths
- Ember Monastery
- Winding Skyroad
- Mirror Summit
- Beacon Cliff

## Navigation Philosophy
The game should feel guided but not narrow. Players should usually know the broad next destination, while still having room for:
- optional side routes
- support-character content
- training and recovery spaces
- hidden or revisitable movement-based discoveries

## Hub And Return Loop
The world should repeatedly alternate between:
- pressure spaces
- support spaces
- confrontation spaces

That gives the structure emotional rhythm. The player does not simply push forward forever. They move outward, break, recover, and rise again.

## Boundary With Long-Term Open-World Direction
The repo's long-term ambition points toward a more systemic world model, but the current GDD should describe the shipped experience first as a campaign-led Metroidvania structure with controlled elasticity, not as a free-roam sandbox.
