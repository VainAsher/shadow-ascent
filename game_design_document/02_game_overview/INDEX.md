## Working Questions
- What kind of game is this, in player-facing terms?
- Who is it for?
- How guided or open should it feel?
- What emotional promise does the first hour make?

## High-Risk Drift Areas
- audience claims outrunning real evidence
- open-world language overstating the shipped structure
- tone drifting away from the wholeness/loss/support spine
