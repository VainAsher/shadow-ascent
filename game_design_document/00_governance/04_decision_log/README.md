# Decision Log

## Purpose
Record major design decisions, alternatives considered, and final rationale.

## Working Rule
This folder should track more than final answers. It should also record critical reviews of the GDD when those reviews reveal:
- unresolved design choices
- scope risk
- weak implementation handoff areas
- sections that need research before they should be treated as approved direction

## What Belongs Here
- major design decisions and rationale
- review notes that materially change how the GDD should be read
- source-backed critiques that expose gaps or contradictions
- links to the exact GDD sections affected by a review
