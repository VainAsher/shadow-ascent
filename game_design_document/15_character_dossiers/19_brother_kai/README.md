# Brother Kai

## Core Identity
- Brother Kai is the motion-forward monastery support who pushes Aen into action when freezing becomes a habit.

## Narrative Function
- Kai makes support feel active and physical, not only gentle or reflective.

## Concrete Behaviors
- runs action-first drills that break hesitation
- frames breath-through-action and movement-through-fear as trainable
- supports challenge rooms where motion must resume under pressure

## Gameplay Impact
- gives Ember Monastery training a more forceful tempo
- can support movement drills, pressure exercises, or action-restart sequences
- helps differentiate kinds of support inside the monastery cast

## System Dependencies
- monastery training spaces
- movement drill scripting
- support dialogue

## Failure Mode
- Kai fails if he overlaps Roga entirely and adds no distinct action-first support energy.
