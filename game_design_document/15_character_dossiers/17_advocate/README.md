# Advocate

## Core Identity
- Advocate is the defended-dignity figure who helps separate understanding harm from excusing it.

## Narrative Function
- The Advocate prevents the game from collapsing grief into self-blame or moral fog.

## Concrete Behaviors
- names what happened to Aen as wrong rather than merely tragic
- challenges cold judgement, especially where systems or institutions deny lived harm
- supports dialogue or quest beats that reject internalized blame

## Gameplay Impact
- strengthens moral framing in dialogue-heavy or judgement-heavy sequences
- can influence resolution of institutional or record-based spaces like Stone Judge logic
- helps the player read some conflicts as dignity defense rather than mere obstacle clearing

## System Dependencies
- dialogue/state framing
- judgement/institution quest beats
- consequence or moral-resolution systems

## Failure Mode
- Advocate fails if they become pure exposition instead of an active counterweight to blame.
