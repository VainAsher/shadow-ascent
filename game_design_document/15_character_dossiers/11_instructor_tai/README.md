# Instructor Tai

## Core Identity
- Instructor Tai is Aen's first formal teacher: a disciplined village mentor who teaches form before trauma redefines what discipline means.

## Narrative Function
- Tai establishes baseline training, rhythm, and responsibility in Lantern Heights.
- He gives the player a pre-wound model of skill that later mentors can deepen rather than replace.

## Concrete Behaviors
- teaches basic movement drills, timing, and dojo etiquette
- frames early sparring and control before real collapse enters the campaign
- warns against shortcuts, empty pride, or confusing approval with readiness

## Gameplay Impact
- anchors the first movement tutorial and early combat discipline
- gives early tutorial spaces emotional legitimacy instead of making them feel abstract
- makes post-Hollowing growth feel like a continuation of form under pressure, not totally new training

## System Dependencies
- opening tutorial content
- dojo scripting and early dialogue
- beginner movement/combat onboarding

## Failure Mode
- Tai fails if he becomes a generic tutorial NPC with no influence on how early play feels.
