# Mentor Roga

## Core Identity
- Mentor Roga is the endurance-and-discipline support pillar: a teacher whose authority comes from presence inside pain, not from distance from it.
- He should feel demanding, calm, and exacting without becoming cruel.
- His support style is training that proves discomfort can be survived and shaped.

## Narrative Function
- Roga turns endurance into a recoverable skill rather than a personality trait.
- He anchors the part of Aen's rebuilding arc that converts survival into disciplined mastery.
- He is the clearest character bridge between pain as something suffered and pain as something one can move within without surrender.

## What They Represent
- Roga represents endurance, wall-cling mastery, disciplined breath, and presence inside hardship.
- He represents mentorship that neither flatters nor abandons.
- He also represents the truth that some forms of recovery are repetitive, technical, and earned against resistance.

## How They Express That In Play
- Roga's dojo content should stage endurance drills that ask the player to stay with difficulty instead of brute-forcing around it.
- Wall-cling mastery should belong to him explicitly. His teaching should make hard surfaces and vertical waits feel like part of movement language, not accidental stall states.
- Trials tied to Roga should reinforce that falling, slipping, and trying again are part of the lesson. His presence should calm shame without reducing challenge.
- He should appear most clearly where Aen must learn to remain inside pressure: vertical chambers, breath-timed climbs, and precision recovery after mistakes.

## Gameplay Impact
- Roga's dossier should directly affect vertical traversal mastery, wall-cling feel, and the player's interpretation of repeated failure.
- His training should convert frustrating friction into intentional technique, especially in dojo and ascent spaces.
- He keeps endurance distinct from Samson's courage and Marcel's reinforcement by making persistence itself playable.
- His presence also helps the game teach that pain can be inhabited without becoming identity.

## Player Learning
- The player learns that mastery often looks like calm repetition rather than dramatic breakthrough.
- Roga teaches that staying on the wall, holding position, and breathing through pressure are part of progress.
- His content teaches the player how to fail without reading failure as personal collapse.

## Act-by-Act Presence
- Early game: Roga is established as a serious mentor figure, but not yet a central support driver.
- Post-Hollowing and Ember Monastery: Roga becomes active through dojo training and wall-cling mastery.
- Winding Skyroad: his lessons pay off in sustained vertical ascent under pressure.
- Mirror Summit and Beacon lead-up: his contribution persists as the player's capacity to hold form under stress.
- Ending read: Roga's thread confirms that discipline helped make release possible.

## System Dependencies
- Dojo challenge scripting and progression flags.
- Wall-cling, wall-jump, and vertical traversal tuning.
- Failure-state iteration that supports repeated attempts without tonal collapse.
- Dialogue and encounter framing that distinguish disciplined pressure from humiliation.

## Failure Mode
- Roga fails if he becomes a generic hard mentor who only increases difficulty.
- He also fails if wall-cling mastery is not concretely tied to his content.
- If the player cannot feel the difference between cruel punishment and trained persistence, the dossier is not doing its job.
