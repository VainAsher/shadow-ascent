## Drift Risks
- Marcel becomes a generic blacksmith if repair and reinforcement are not tied to consequence.
- Gear support loses meaning if survivability reads like a reset instead of recovery with scars.
- He overlaps Hazel if "care" is not distinguished from material maintenance and durability.
- He overlaps Roga if toughness through forging is not separated from toughness through endurance training.
