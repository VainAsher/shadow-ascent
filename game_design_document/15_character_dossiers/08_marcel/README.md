# Marcel

## Core Identity
- Marcel is the repair-and-reinforcement support pillar: a craftsperson who understands that damaged things can be rebuilt without pretending they were never damaged.
- He should feel practical, steady, and materially intelligent.
- His support style is tangible improvement, structural honesty, and durable care.

## Narrative Function
- Marcel turns repair into gameplay consequence.
- He proves that recovery is not only emotional or spiritual; it also has a physical, crafted, maintained dimension.
- He helps the game avoid treating gear as disposable numbers by tying equipment state to meaning.

## What They Represent
- Marcel represents reforging, reinforcement, repair logic, and unbroken will without denial.
- He represents care expressed through labor, craft, and maintenance.
- He also represents the idea that scars can become structure rather than shame.

## How They Express That In Play
- Marcel-related scenes should appear after the Hollowing when Aen's tools, armor, or combat readiness no longer feel intact.
- His questline should use forging and reinforcement as concrete loops: gather needed materials, return to the forge, and convert damaged readiness into sturdier but visibly altered gear.
- Repairs should preserve meaning. Equipment should not snap back to pristine innocence; it should carry the logic of being rebuilt with intention.
- Marcel's systems should teach players why gear matters. Reinforcement choices, repair benches, and forge upgrades should feel like reclaiming reliability, not chasing abstract item level.

## Gameplay Impact
- Marcel's dossier should affect survivability, gear tuning, and the emotional reading of upgrades.
- Reinforcement should make the player feel more prepared without making the wound disappear from presentation.
- His questline gives material weight to recovery by linking forge work to encounter readiness and long-form persistence.
- He also keeps "repair" distinct from "healing": Marcel restores function and integrity, not innocence.

## Player Learning
- The player learns that repair can add strength without erasing damage history.
- Marcel teaches that maintenance is part of endurance, not busywork.
- His systems teach that crafted support can be as emotionally important as dialogue comfort.

## Act-by-Act Presence
- Lantern Heights: Marcel helps establish that gear, craft, and communal upkeep already matter before collapse.
- Post-Hollowing: his absence helps the player feel the fragility of damaged readiness.
- Ember Monastery support arc: Marcel becomes central through forge, reinforcement, and repair logic.
- Skyroad and pre-final preparation: his work should still matter in how stable Aen feels under pressure.
- Beacon resolution: Marcel's contribution confirms that rebuilt function is part of earned wholeness.

## System Dependencies
- Gear upgrade, repair, reinforcement, and resource loops.
- Visual state changes that preserve scars and repair history.
- Optional quest gating through forge materials or relics.
- Encounter tuning that lets reinforced gear feel meaningful without trivializing challenge.

## Failure Mode
- Marcel fails if he is only a generic blacksmith shop wrapper.
- He also fails if repaired gear reads like a full reset to untouched perfection.
- If the player cannot connect his work to durability, preparedness, and meaning, the dossier has not moved beyond symbolism.
