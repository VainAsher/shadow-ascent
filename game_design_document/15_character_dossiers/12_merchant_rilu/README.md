# Merchant Rilu

## Core Identity
- Merchant Rilu is the marketplace witness who notices community drift through everyday trade and absence patterns.

## Narrative Function
- Rilu helps Lantern Heights feel socially alive and gives early warnings that something is changing before the crisis becomes explicit.

## Concrete Behaviors
- handles supplies, rumors, and small routing help through the market
- notices who stops showing up, what stalls close early, and when lantern goods stop moving normally
- shifts from ordinary commerce to social-warning dialogue as the village empties

## Gameplay Impact
- supports early shop interaction and practical village circulation
- gives the player social evidence that the hub is changing, not just aesthetic dimming
- can route side errands, small material purchases, or warning beats before Summit Shrine

## System Dependencies
- shop/menu access
- hub-state dialogue
- early side-quest routing

## Failure Mode
- Rilu fails if the market feels ornamental and never registers the emotional change in Lantern Heights.
