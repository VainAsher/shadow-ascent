# Hazel

## Core Identity
- Hazel is the warmth-and-return support pillar: a gentle lantern weaver whose care becomes physical space, preparation, and future-facing light.
- She should feel soft without being passive and hopeful without denying what happened.
- Her support style is making return possible.

## Narrative Function
- Hazel turns tenderness into playable infrastructure.
- She carries the emotional line from Lantern Heights warmth to Beacon preparation, proving that homecoming is something built toward, not merely wished for.
- She is essential to making the ending's light feel prepared rather than magically granted.

## What They Represent
- Hazel represents warmth, safe return, lantern silk, hospitality, and the future Aen is learning to carry.
- She represents care that leaves the door lit even when reunion is not immediate.
- She also represents hope expressed through preparation instead of fantasy.

## How They Express That In Play
- In Lantern Heights, Hazel should help establish warmth through textiles, lantern imagery, and spaces that feel safe to exhale inside.
- After the wound, Hazel-related content should define or improve safe-return spaces. Each lantern-silk hand-in should reweave one class of refuge element: bedroll warmth, lantern wraps, or doorway drapes that convert a neutral checkpoint into a true recovery room with stronger light, calmer ambience, and full rest identity.
- Lantern silk should be concrete. Collecting it and returning it through Hazel's questline should upgrade recovery-space warmth tiers, unlock Beacon-cloth weaving steps, and prepare the Beacon Lantern assembly in a fixed sequence rather than as generic flavor.
- Her late-game role should culminate in Beacon preparation, where the player completes the final lantern-silk weave, fits the Beacon wrap, and feels that future warmth has been assembled from carried materials, remembered care, and intentional readiness.

## Gameplay Impact
- Hazel's dossier should affect rest-space feel, safe-return readability, warmth-tier upgrades, and the final Beacon setup.
- Lantern silk should matter as more than a collectible by tying soft material care to protection, preparation, and future light systems through explicit turn-ins, refuge upgrades, and Beacon assembly milestones.
- Her support keeps warmth mechanical: the player feels where it is safe to settle, prepare, and continue.
- She also prevents the ending from feeling abrupt by making Beacon activation the endpoint of a tenderness-based support thread.

## Player Learning
- The player learns that gentleness can prepare action rather than interrupt it.
- Hazel teaches that safe return is built through repeated small acts of care.
- Her systems teach that hope is most believable when it changes the space around the player.

## Act-by-Act Presence
- Lantern Heights: Hazel establishes home warmth and the language of lantern-made safety.
- Summit Shrine and Hollow Depths: her absence deepens how cold and function-only the world can feel.
- Ember Monastery support arc: Hazel returns through lantern silk, restored warmth, and preparations that point beyond survival.
- Skyroad and Beacon lead-up: her contribution becomes explicitly future-facing.
- Beacon Cliff: Hazel's thread resolves in the Beacon Lantern and the possibility of homecoming without immediate reunion.

## System Dependencies
- Safe-room or hub-comfort presentation.
- Lantern silk collection, turn-in, refuge-upgrade, and Beacon-preparation logic.
- Beacon Lantern assembly and final preparation systems.
- Environmental storytelling that distinguishes warm kept spaces from neutral checkpoints.

## Failure Mode
- Hazel fails if she is reduced to generic comfort or romance-coded softness.
- She also fails if lantern silk and Beacon preparation do not change anything the player can feel.
- If her warmth is only spoken about and never spatially expressed, the dossier is incomplete.
