# Smith Jenro

## Core Identity
- Smith Jenro is the older craft authority who reads weakness through tools, posture, and over-polished surfaces.

## Narrative Function
- Jenro gives Lantern Heights a deeper craft tradition than Marcel alone and serves as an early-warning voice about false strength.

## Concrete Behaviors
- comments on weapons, fittings, and posture with practical metaphors
- notices when Aen's handling changes under the influence of praise or strain
- distinguishes real strength from bright polish in pre-collapse dialogue and training context

## Gameplay Impact
- enriches early forge/world credibility before Marcel's repair loop becomes central
- helps foreshadow that looking chosen is not the same as being stable
- can support small inspection beats, tool checks, or pre-collapse gear conversations

## System Dependencies
- village dialogue
- early forge/worldbuilding interactions
- pre-collapse warning content

## Failure Mode
- Jenro fails if he duplicates Marcel without adding elder craft judgment and warning logic.
