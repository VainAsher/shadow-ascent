# Tier 1 Character Dossiers

This document contains the character dossiers for Tier 1 characters in Shadow Ascent.

## Overview

The Tier 1 characters are the core supporting characters that interact with the player during the game's narrative. These characters have been developed with detailed personality profiles, abilities, and narrative arcs.

## Included Characters

- **01_village_mentor** - The player's guide at the village
- **02_raven_speaker** - A mysterious oracle who provides prophecies
- **03_guard_warden** - A loyal protector with a tragic backstory
- **04_healer_haunt** - A mysterious entity bound to the land
- **05_wandering_poet** - A storyteller who reveals truths through song

## Progress

These dossiers are being actively developed. Some characters are complete while others are in various stages of development.

## Usage

When referencing these characters in design documents, code comments, or team discussions, always refer to them by their numeric identifier (e.g., "Village Mentor" or "01") rather than their full name to maintain consistency throughout development.
