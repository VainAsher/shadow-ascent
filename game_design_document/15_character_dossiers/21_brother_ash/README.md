# Brother Ash

## Core Identity
- Brother Ash is the grief-aware support figure whose authority comes partly from surviving failure himself.

## Narrative Function
- Ash keeps monastery support from reading as flawless or untouched by pain.

## Concrete Behaviors
- speaks from prior failure, setback, or grief rather than polished certainty
- normalizes relapse, strain, and imperfect rebuilding
- supports training or dialogue beats where endurance is reframed as continuing after failure

## Gameplay Impact
- deepens monastery support credibility by showing that helpers are not idealized abstractions
- can support recovery-after-failure loops, repeat-attempt framing, or dialogue after setbacks
- helps the player read mistakes as part of growth rather than disqualification

## System Dependencies
- monastery dialogue
- retry/failure framing
- support-state scenes

## Failure Mode
- Ash fails if monastery support feels too clean and untested by real grief.
