# Lantern Kid

## Core Identity
- Lantern Kid is the innocence-and-inheritance figure who shows what Aen's survival protects for the future.

## Narrative Function
- The child perspective keeps the ending future-facing instead of purely retrospective.

## Concrete Behaviors
- admires Aen innocently in early Lantern Heights scenes
- helps the village feel lived in through imitation, small errands, or lantern fascination
- reappears later as proof that what Aen survives can become shelter for someone else

## Gameplay Impact
- gives early home scenes a future-facing stake
- can support small village interactions, light tutorial charm, or end-state emotional payoffs
- reinforces that Beacon hope matters beyond Aen's private healing

## System Dependencies
- village NPC scripting
- light social interaction
- ending or return-state dialogue

## Failure Mode
- Lantern Kid fails if the future has no face and the Beacon feels only symbolic.
