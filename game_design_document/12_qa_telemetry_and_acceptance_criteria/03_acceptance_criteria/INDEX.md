# Acceptance Criteria Index

## Checklist
- [ ] Keep criteria tied to player-observable outcomes
- [ ] Separate movement, combat, narrative, and procgen gates clearly
- [ ] Review criteria at milestone boundaries and after major scope changes
- [ ] Tighten vague wording into pass or fail language when the build matures

## Drift Risks
- Vague acceptance criteria will not help triage quality disputes
- Overly rigid criteria too early can create fake certainty
