# Acceptance Criteria

## Core Acceptance Frame
The game should be judged on whether its movement, combat, narrative, and procgen each meet observable quality gates.

## Strong Current Acceptance Ideas
- movement becomes understandable quickly
- each major ability has a clear, readable use case
- the critical path avoids frame-perfect requirements
- armed and unarmed expression are both viable
- the scripted loss feels motivated
- Hollow Depths feel heavy without becoming tedious
- Ember Monastery feels supportive without becoming falsely cheerful
- generated critical rooms always pass reachability validation

## Critical Review Note
These are good design intentions, but several are not yet measurable enough to act as signoff gates.

## Further Development Needed
Turn the strongest items into pass/fail checks, for example:
- how quickly new players should complete the opening movement tutorial
- what counts as a readable use case for each major ability
- what maximum precision burden is acceptable on the critical path
- what playtest evidence distinguishes “heavy” from “tedious” in Hollow Depths

## Designer Questions
- Which 5 acceptance checks matter most for the next playable milestone?
- What can be validated by direct observation versus telemetry?
- Which emotional criteria need player interview evidence instead of binary pass/fail checks?
