# Appearance

## Aen
Aen should begin with village warmth in his palette: muted charcoal or practical ninja tones with gold or lantern-warm accents. After the Hollowing, those accents should feel drained, broken, or spiritually interrupted. By the end, his design should read calm rather than flashy. Whole Aen should not look royal or over-upgraded. He should look like someone who no longer needs to prove he has power.

## Yin and Yang
- **Yin**: pale blue-white or silver-white orb, soft trailing mist, rounded motion, gentle hover behavior
- **Yang**: gold-white orb, brighter sparks, sharper arcs, quicker movement

They should read as spiritual truths externalized, not as decorative pets.

## Veil Maiden / Siren of Masks
The Veil Maiden should use pale silk, silver light, mask motifs, ceremonial stillness, and too-perfect symmetry. The Siren reveal should turn admiration and beauty into a cage of faces, performance, and possession.

## Core Support Cast
- **Samson**: strong silhouette, spear, wraps, shoulder guard, grounded red or bronze courage accents
- **Sophia**: ink, folded maps, star charts, ribbons, tools, indigo or parchment tones with silver or teal detail
- **Marcel**: forge gloves, scarf or shoulder plating, patched armor, soot, iron and ember palette
- **Hazel**: lantern-silk ribbons, woven shawl, satchel of thread or charms, soft amber warmth

## Mentors and Support NPCs
- **Instructor Tai**: clean practical training robes, staff or wooden sword, lantern-school insignia
- **Smith Jenro**: weathered craftsperson, heavy gloves, scars, calm soot-dark palette
- **Shade Hermit**: cave-shadow cloth, pale eyes, root or broken-lantern details
- **Smith Monk**: ember beads, soot-stained robes, quiet forge-and-monastery hybrid
- **Listening Elder**: seated grace, lantern bowl, prayer beads, listening motifs
- **Advocate**: upright formal cloth, scroll cases, bold markings, assertive silhouette
- **Hearth Brothers**: shared monastery palette with distinct silhouettes
- **Mentor Roga**: compact, scarred, climbing wraps, vertical-training visual language

## Hollow Reflection
The Hollow Reflection should be recognizable as Aen but wrong: darker silhouette, missing warmth, cracked spirit-space where Yin and Yang once orbited, mask-like facial read, and movement trails that make familiarity feel threatening.
