# Relationships

## Core Relationship Rule
The cast should not read as a quest checklist. Each major character represents a different relationship Aen has to healing, truth, praise, support, endurance, or release.

## Aen and Yin / Yang
Yin and Yang are Aen's visible spiritual truth. Their relationship to him is not ownership. It is witness, balance, and proof of original wholeness.

## Aen and the Veil Maiden
This is the central toxic relationship in the game. She mistakes possession for recognition and teaches Aen to confuse being specially chosen with being truly loved.

## Aen and Samson
Samson is brotherhood and honest courage. He loves Aen before the myth and is willing to challenge him rather than let him disappear into manipulation.

## Aen and Sophia
Sophia restores orientation. Her relationship to Aen is built on truth, pattern, memory, and the possibility that the world still has shape after distortion.

## Aen and Marcel
Marcel represents repair without denial. He helps Aen understand that being repaired does not make him false or lesser.

## Aen and Hazel
Hazel is warmth without demand. She represents the possibility of future homecoming without requiring that the past be restored intact.

## Aen and Mentors
- **Tai** teaches early discipline before trauma.
- **Roga** teaches endurance after trauma.
- **Smith Monk**, **Listening Elder**, **Advocate**, and the **Hearth Brothers** show that healing is social, embodied, and structured in multiple forms.

## Aen and Hollow Reflection
This is the final relationship question of the game. The Hollow Reflection is not just an enemy to be beaten. It is the wounded self asking whether survival hardened into identity. The best ending comes when Aen releases that version of himself instead of simply erasing it.
