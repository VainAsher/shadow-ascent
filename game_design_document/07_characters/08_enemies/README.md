# Enemies

## Design Principle
Major enemies should embody forms of emotional and psychological pressure, not exist only as stat checks.

## Spatial Rule
Enemies should also be designed as space problems before they are damage sponges. Each enemy should answer:
- what space do I threaten
- what movement answer solves me
- what emotional pressure do I represent

That framing is especially important for a game where traversal and combat are meant to stay fused.

## Symbolic Functions
- **Siren of Masks** represents false praise, possession, and the seduction of specialness.
- **Weightbound Ogre** represents exhaustion, heaviness, and the crushing labor of surviving when every step feels too much.
- **Shatter Moth Queen** represents gaslighting, poisoned self-trust, false paths, and emotional hostility.
- **Stone Judge** represents rigid systems, cold judgement, and terrifying indifference.
- **Hollow Reflection** represents Aen's own rage, grief, emptiness, obsession, and the belief that he can only be whole again by reclaiming what was taken.

## Encounter Reading
- **Siren of Masks** should begin beautiful, controlled, and validating before revealing admiration as a cage.
- **Weightbound Ogre** should feel slow, crushing, and bodily exhausting rather than theatrically angry.
- **Shatter Moth Queen** should distort perception through false safety, broken trust, and unstable reality.
- **Stone Judge** should feel less like a monster and more like an institution that refuses to see Aen.
- **Hollow Reflection** should move like Aen with the meaning of every move turned inward and hostile.

## Boss Rule
Bosses should function as emotional states embodied mechanically. They are not just difficulty spikes; they are movement examinations, psychological confrontations, and narrative mirrors.

## Final Boss Rule
The Siren causes the wound, but the Hollow Reflection is the true final boss. The deepest enemy in the story is not the manipulator alone. It is what the wound becomes inside Aen afterward.

## Combat Expression Note
The final encounter should mirror the player's own recovered toolkit. The strongest canonical resolution comes from release and balanced expression rather than simple domination.
