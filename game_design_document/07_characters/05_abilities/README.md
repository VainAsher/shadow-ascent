# Abilities

## Aen's Abilities
Aen's mechanical progression should remain readable as emotional progression. The current game structure already supports a stateful movement arc from basic capability to rebuilt movement to full-kit wholeness.

## Thematic Ability Provenance
- **Dash**: movement returns through practical support
- **Double Jump**: the second reach after collapse
- **Glide**: surviving the fall rather than denying it
- **Air Dodge**: support creating room to breathe
- **Grapple**: accepting help and being pulled upward
- **Wall Cling / Wall Jump mastery**: endurance and contact with pain without immediate collapse
- **Beacon Lantern**: warmth and future, not reunion

## Combat Expression
Aen armed should often read as fear, urgency, or survival. Aen unarmed should read as restraint, acceptance, and self-mastery. This distinction is especially important for the final confrontation.

## Spirit Abilities
Yin and Yang should not be framed as simple utility companions. Their presence early in the game proves spiritual abundance. Their absence later changes Aen's state rather than becoming a standard recoverable power bar.

## Boss Ability Reading
The Hollow Reflection should mirror Aen's full toolkit, but twist its meaning:
- Dash becomes avoidance
- Grapple becomes control
- Wall Cling becomes refusal to let go
- Glide becomes numb drift
- Double Jump becomes desperate overreach

## Support Cast Function
Many support characters should not grant abstract upgrades in a vacuum. Their gifts should feel narratively grounded:
- Smith Monk enabling Dash
- Sophia restoring truthful orientation
- Marcel reframing repair and durability
- Hazel tying warmth to the Beacon
- Hearth Brothers and Roga turning support into embodied mastery
