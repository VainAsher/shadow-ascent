## Character Functions
- **Aen** is the protagonist and emotional vessel of the game. His flaw is not weakness but confusion: he mistakes praise for love and isolation for destiny.
- **Yin** represents stillness, intuition, softness, and restraint.
- **Yang** represents courage, action, boldness, and fire.
- **Veil Maiden / Siren of Masks** weaponizes admiration, false understanding, and possession. She does not simply attack Aen; she isolates him by making him feel uniquely chosen.
- **Hollow Reflection** is the real final enemy because it is Aen's pain, emptiness, rage, and obsession taking his shape.

## Support Network
The supporting cast should not feel ornamental. They are parts of Aen's recovery structure:
- **Samson** represents brotherhood, courage, sparring, and loyal presence without performance.
- **Sophia** represents maps, truth, naming, and remembering clearly.
- **Marcel** represents reforging and the idea that broken things can become stronger without pretending they were never broken.
- **Hazel** represents gentleness, lantern silk, future warmth, and the seed of the Beacon.
- **Instructor Tai**, **Mentor Roga**, the **Hearth Brothers**, **Shade Hermit**, **Listening Elder**, and **Advocate** all contribute different forms of discipline, witness, or practical support.

## Design Rule
The story is strongest when Aen is not healed by invincibility or solitary destiny. He is healed because he stops being alone and learns that wholeness does not depend on possession.
