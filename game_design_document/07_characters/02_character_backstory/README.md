# Character Backstory

## Aen
Aen's core truth is that he was never incomplete. He begins life already carrying both Yin and Yang, which makes him spiritually unusual from the start. His wound is not missing power. His wound is believing he needs external validation to be worthy of love and safety.

## Yin and Yang
Yin and Yang are not ordinary companion creatures. They are Aen's visible spiritual truth. Their presence in Act 0 proves he was "more than whole" from the beginning. Their loss at Summit Shrine is therefore not just a mechanical setback but a spiritual amputation.

## Veil Maiden / Siren of Masks
The Veil Maiden enters Aen's life as someone who appears to understand him. She flatters rather than threatens. Her backstory function is not brute evil but manipulation through chosen-ness, sacred destiny, and the promise of being specially seen.

## Support Cast
The major support cast should each feel like they existed in Aen's life before the wound in a way that makes their later loss, return, or echo emotionally legible:
- Samson knows Aen before the myth.
- Sophia gives the world shape and record.
- Marcel represents practical repair.
- Hazel represents warmth that does not demand performance.
- Tai represents ordinary discipline before trauma.

## Hollow Reflection
The Hollow Reflection is not a separate villain with a separate history. It is the end-state of Aen's pain if grief, rage, survival, and obsession harden into identity.
