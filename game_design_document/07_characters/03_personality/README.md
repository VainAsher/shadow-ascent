# Personality

## Aen
Aen should begin soft but capable, admired but emotionally vulnerable. He is not arrogant in a simple way. He is susceptible to praise because he wants to believe being chosen means being safe. Over the campaign, he should become calmer, clearer, and less driven by the need to prove himself.

## Yin
Yin should feel quiet, reflective, patient, compassionate, inward, and moonlike. Yin's emotional language is stillness, sorrow, intuition, rest, and acceptance.

## Yang
Yang should feel warm, active, protective, brave, outward, and sunlike. Yang's emotional language is courage, motion, hope, and forward force.

## Veil Maiden / Siren of Masks
She should be beautiful, controlled, and subtly wrong. Her danger is soft attention. She makes isolation sound noble and dependence sound sacred. As the Siren, that false care turns into possession.

## Samson
Samson is direct, protective, grounded, and emotionally honest. He is the friend who would rather challenge Aen than watch him disappear into someone else's story about him.

## Sophia
Sophia is observant, precise, quietly brave, and emotionally intelligent. She does not restore truth through grand speeches. She restores it through memory, pattern, and evidence.

## Marcel
Marcel is steady, practical, and physically warm. He may be less verbally expressive than Samson or Sophia, but his care is visible in what he repairs, carries, and strengthens.

## Hazel
Hazel is gentle without being fragile. She offers warmth without demanding performance and becomes increasingly important because she understands return, not reversal.

## Monastery Support Figures
- Shade Hermit: strange, quiet witness of survival
- Smith Monk: practical, non-invasive support through action
- Listening Elder: validating presence without pressure
- Advocate: defended dignity and moral clarity
- Hearth Brothers: support made communal and repeatable
- Mentor Roga: demanding without cruelty, endurance without denial
