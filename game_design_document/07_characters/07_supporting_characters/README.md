# Supporting Characters

## Design Rule
Supporting characters should each represent a specific kind of relationship to healing, truth, warning, memory, survival, or return. They are not filler NPCs.

## Lantern Heights Support Cast
- **Instructor Tai**: discipline before trauma; teaches skill and baseline form
- **Merchant Rilu**: everyday life, social warning, and the marketplace sensing community drift first
- **Smith Jenro**: older craft tradition, early warning, and the difference between polish and strength
- **Old Man Riku**: village memory and who Aen was before the wound
- **Lantern Kid**: innocence, inheritance, and the future Aen's survival protects

## Core Companions
- **Samson**: brotherhood, sparring honesty, courage
- **Sophia**: truth, maps, memory, orientation
- **Marcel**: repair, reinforcement, unbroken will
- **Hazel**: gentle warmth, lantern silk, future homecoming

## Hollow Depths Support
- **Shade Hermit**: witness of survival without false healing
- **Smith Monk**: practical support and the return of motion
- **Listening Elder**: validation without pressure
- **Advocate**: defended dignity and the refusal to excuse harm

## Ember Monastery Support
- **Hearth Brother**: warm welcome into a structure of care
- **Brother Kai**: physical push into motion
- **Brother Len**: timing, breath, patience
- **Brother Ash**: support from someone who also knows failure or grief
- **Mentor Roga**: endurance, presence inside pain, disciplined mastery

## Functional Rule
Support characters should not just hand out upgrades or exposition. Their gameplay contribution should reinforce their emotional function:
- warnings should feel social
- map help should restore truth
- repair should reframe damage
- warmth should make future possible
- training should become embodied healing
